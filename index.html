About LinkShort

LinkShort is a privacy-first, ad-free link shortener that works entirely in your browser using LocalStorage.

No servers, no tracking — everything is stored locally on your device.

You can create up to 10 links per browser account.

All your links and view counts are stored privately and securely inside your browser’s storage.
