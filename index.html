About LinkShort

LinkShort is a privacy-first, ad-free link shortener that stores all your data locally using LocalStorage.

No ads. No servers. Everything stays in your browser.

You can create up to 10 links per account, stored locally in your browser’s memory.

Your links, names, and view counts remain private and accessible only to you on this device.
